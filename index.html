About Delta Marine

A local marine surveying practice with a long-standing presence.

Established in 1991, Delta Marine Services is a Mangalore-based marine surveying firm providing marine surveying and loss assessment, marine and cargo superintendence, and specialist inspection services.

With its roots in coastal Karnataka, the company supports maritime requirements in Mangalore and can discuss assignments at other Indian ports, subject to the scope and availability of each assignment.

Our Approach

Clear scope.
Careful inspection.
Useful reporting.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Discuss the requirement

02 / INSPECT

Conduct the survey

03 / DOCUMENT

Record the findings
